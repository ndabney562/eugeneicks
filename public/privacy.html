Privacy Policy

EUGENEICKS · Effective November 1, 2026

The short version: EUGENEICKS does not sell or share personal data. The only thing it sends anywhere is your score, under the leaderboard name you pick, to the browser game's public leaderboard. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, in the iPhone app or in the browser.

What stays on your device

Your saves stay on your own iPhone or in your own browser and are never sent to us. They hold your settings, records, unlocked Pals, achievements and a paused shift. Deleting the app, or clearing the site's data in your browser, erases them.

Game Center (iPhone app)

If you are signed in to Game Center, the app sends your best score for each difficulty and the achievements you unlock to Apple's Game Center, so they appear on its leaderboards and in your profile. Apple handles that data under Apple's Privacy Policy, and your Game Center settings control who sees your nickname and scores. If you are not signed in, nothing is sent.

The browser version

The browser game is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address when your browser loads the page; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. Besides its online leaderboard (below), the game only asks its own site whether a newer version exists.

Online leaderboard (browser version)

When a shift ends in the browser, the game sends your result to the EUGENEICKS leaderboard so it can be ranked: the leaderboard name you chose ("Dr. Eugene" if you never picked one), your score, round, best streak, germs disinfected, difficulty, mode and the Pal you played. Anyone can see the names and scores on the leaderboard, so don't use a name you'd rather keep private. Nothing else about you or your device is stored with it. The leaderboard runs on Cloudflare, which may log technical data such as your IP address; see the Cloudflare Privacy Policy. To have a score removed, open an issue (link below). The iPhone app doesn't use this leaderboard; it uses Game Center.

Children

EUGENEICKS does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions: open an issue at github.com/ndabney562/eugeneicks/issues.
